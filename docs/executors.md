# Executors

## Codex

`ivoai codex` launches the official client and preserves its ChatGPT subscription login.

## Claude Code

`ivoai claude` launches the official client and preserves its native subscription login.

## OpenCode

`ivoai opencode` launches the pinned managed binary as a direct primary. Its provider
and authentication remain owned by OpenCode. AUTO and worker modes are planned, not shipped.
